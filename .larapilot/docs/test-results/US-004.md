# US-004 — Manual test handoff

Automated coverage (`php artisan test`, `NORMAL` bar) is in `tests/Unit/Calendar/SyncHealthReporterTest.php`, `tests/Feature/SyncResyncTest.php`, and `tests/Feature/SyncStatusTest.php`. No Playwright, Dusk, Pest browser, or viewport-matrix suite exists at this bar — the following need a human pass at `http://personal-kbms.test`.

## Keyboard-only pass

- Tab to the sync pill's "Resync" / "Retry" / "Sync now" control (state-dependent) and activate it with `Enter` or `Space` — confirm the pill updates without a page reload.
- While a run is in progress (`php artisan kbms:sync-calendar`, no `queue:work` running so the row stays `Running`), confirm the resync control is genuinely **absent** from the tab order, not merely disabled.
- Tab to the "Retry now" / "Resync now" button inside the failure/stale banner (when present) and activate it the same way.

## Screen reader / `aria-live` pass

- With a screen reader running, trigger a resync and confirm the pill's state change is announced once (`aria-live="polite"`), not on every 60-second poll tick.
- Confirm the failure banner is announced immediately on load (`role="alert"`) and the stale banner is announced politely (`role="status"`), not as an urgent interruption.

## Reduced motion

- Enable `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` at the OS level, force a `Syncing` state, and confirm the pulsing dot (`kb-pulse`) is neutralised (no visible animation) per the global rule in `resources/css/app.css`.

## Theme pass (`states.html` checklist)

Compare both the pill and the banners against `.larapilot/mockups/kbms-app/states.html` in light and dark:

- All five pill states (`never`, `syncing`, `ok`, `stale`, `failed`) are legible and visually distinct in both themes.
- The failure banner (`--kb-danger` tint) and stale banner (`--kb-warn` tint) stay readable on `--card` in both themes.

## 375 px pass

- Confirm the sync pill does not clip or wrap the header strip at 375 px width, and the failure/stale banner remains fully readable without horizontal scroll.
