Navigation pattern — one per app Hamburger drawer, left-anchored, scrim over the content. It is the Starter Kit's own sidebar sheet: below 768 px .sk-sidebar is hidden and .sk-sidebar-toggle appears in the header. When open it is a modal dialog — focus moves into the panel, Esc and the scrim both close it, focus returns to the trigger. No bottom bar, no tab bar; two destinations do not justify one.
What reflows, what does not Agenda rows drop from three columns to a stack (time inline above the title, badges left-aligned). The meeting detail's two columns collapse to one with the mirrored feed panel first, so the read-only context is read before the editors. The month grid keeps seven columns and gains overflow-x-auto — a calendar with fewer than seven day columns stops being a calendar, so this is the one place horizontal scroll is accepted. Event time labels drop out of month cells below 768 px; the accessible name keeps them.
Touch targets Below 768 px every .kb-iconbtn, .sk-btn and segmented-control button grows to a 44 px minimum height. Calendar event chips stay small by necessity in month view — that is why the day cell itself is also tappable and week/day views exist.

Breakpoints verified: 320 (agenda and detail readable, month grid scrolls), 375 (frames above), 768 (sidebar returns as a drawer trigger, detail still single column), 1024 (detail becomes two columns, sidebar pinned), 1280 (primary design width), 1920 (content capped at 88 rem, left-aligned, no stretched line lengths). Portrait and landscape differ only in available height; nothing is height-dependent.
