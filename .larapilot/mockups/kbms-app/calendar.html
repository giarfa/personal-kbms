Mockup annotation — keyboard & screen reader (US-008) FullCalendar's defaults are not sufficient on their own. Every event must be a real focusable link whose accessible name carries time, title and coverage — the marks are aria-hidden decoration on top of that name, never the only signal. The grid needs arrow-key movement between days plus Home/End, the view switcher is a pressed-state button group, and view + date position must survive a round trip to a meeting and back.
